Works inside Claude Science

CiteProof also ships as a local MCP server. Your AI agent does the thinking; CiteProof keeps the record straight.

  • Stores every review decision in a per-review local database
  • Counts PRISMA numbers and checks that they add up
  • Checks quotes against the source text you supply
  • Records screening decisions and computes reviewer agreement (Cohen's kappa)
  • Finds duplicate records and clusters them
  • Checks DOIs and flags retractions through Crossref
  • Exports an audit bundle you can keep with the review

The server contains no language model. The agent makes every judgement; CiteProof stores it and checks it where it can.

How it works

A structured four-stage research workflow built for reproducible, citation-grounded literature synthesis.

01

Search

Query multiple academic indexes—including OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and arXiv—simultaneously from a single research question.

02

Screen with PRISMA counts

Deduplicate papers by DOI and normalized title, then score abstracts against research criteria on a 0–10 relevance scale while tracking identification, deduplication, screening, and inclusion numbers.

03

Extract evidence

Extract key findings, methodologies, domain frameworks, and research gaps from the screened corpus into a structured evidence table.

04

Write and check citations

Draft structured review sections with claims evaluated against source text by a model, and unresolved or retracted citations flagged before export.

Evidence and citation checks

Honest validation at every step. Model judgements are backed by extracted source passages.

DOI and retraction check

Resolves identifiers against Crossref and DataCite to confirm paper records exist, identify duplicates, and flag retracted literature before synthesis.

Claim-support check by a model

Every generated claim is checked by a model against retrieved full-text evidence. Unsupported statements are flagged or rewritten before assembly.

Evidence table

Assembles a structured matrix tabulating study authors, methodology, empirical findings, and evidence levels for transparent auditability.

Prose lint

Scans draft sections for ungrounded generalizations, vague quantifiers and process narration before assembling the final document.
